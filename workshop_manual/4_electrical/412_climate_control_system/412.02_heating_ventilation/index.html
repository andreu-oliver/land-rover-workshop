Blower

Removal

  1. Remove the air inlet duct.
    For additional information, refer to Air Inlet Duct (80.15.31)

  2. CAUTION: Make sure that all openings are sealed. Use new blanking caps.

    Disconnect the coolant expansion tank pipe.

    • Release the clip.
    Coolant expansion tank pipe clip (E89352)
  3. Disconnect the recirculation blend door control cable.
    • Release the clip.
    Recirculation blend door control cable clip (E89353)
  4. Disconnect the blower motor resistor electrical connector.
    5. Blower motor resistor electrical connector (E89354)
  5. Disconnect the blower motor electrical connector.
Blower motor electrical connector (E89355)
  1. Remove the blower.
    • Remove the 3 nuts.
    Blower nuts (E89356)

Installation

  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
    • Tighten to 15 Nm (11 lb.ft).
    Blower installation (E89356)

Blower Motor (80.20.15)

Removal

NOTE: LHD shown, RHD similar.
  1. Release the coolant expansion tank to radiator pipe.
    2. Coolant expansion tank to radiator pipe (E88943)
  2. Release the coolant expansion tank.
    • Remove the bolt.
    Coolant expansion tank bolt (E88944)
  3. Disconnect the blower motor electrical connector.
    4. Blower motor electrical connector (E88945)
  4. Remove the blower motor.
    • Remove the 3 screws.
Blower motor screws (E88946)

Installation

  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
    • Apply a bead of sealant to the mating faces of the blower motor and blower motor housing.
  2. Tighten to 10 Nm (7 lb.ft).
    3. Blower motor installation (E88944)

Heater Core (80.20.29)

Removal

  1. Disconnect the battery ground cable.
    For additional information, refer to Battery Disconnect and Connect
  2. Remove the heater core housing.
    For additional information, refer to Heater Core Housing

  3. CAUTION: Make sure no damage occurs to the foam seal on the heater core. Failure to follow this caution may result in damage to the component.

    Remove the heater core.

    • Remove the screw.
    • Remove the pipe clamp.
    Heater core removal (E88665)
  4. NOTE: Do not disassemble further if the component is removed for access only.

    Remove the grommet from the heater core pipes.

Installation

  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
  2. Connect the battery ground cable.
    For additional information, refer to Battery Connect

Heater Core and Evaporator Core Housing

  1. Disconnect the battery ground cable.
    For additional information, refer to Battery Disconnect and Connect
  2. Recover the air conditioning (A/C) refrigerant.

  3. Remove the engine control module (ECM).
    For additional information, refer to Engine Control Module (ECM) (18.30.03)

  4. CAUTION: Make sure that all openings are sealed. Use new blanking caps.

    Release the thermostatic expansion valve manifold and tube assembly.

    • Remove the bolt.
    • Remove and discard the O-ring seals.
    Thermostatic expansion valve manifold and tube assembly (E88649)
  5. Disconnect the heater core hoses.
    • Using a suitable pipe clamp, clamp the heater hoses.
    • Release the 2 clips.
    Heater core hoses clips (E88648)
  6. Remove the instrument panel.
    For additional information, refer to Instrument Panel (76.46.23/99)
  7. NOTE: Make sure the clips are discarded.

    Remove the audio unit support bracket.

  • Remove and discard the 5 clips.
Audio unit support bracket clips (E88598)
  1. Remove the LH demister duct.
    • Remove the clip.
    LH demister duct clip (E88632)
  2. Release the LH defrost vent duct.
    • Remove the screw.
    LH defrost vent duct screw (E88633)
  3. Remove the LH defrost vent duct.
    • Remove the clip.
    LH defrost vent duct clip (E88634)
  1. Remove the RH demister duct.
    • Remove the clip.
    RH demister duct clip (E88635)
  2. Release the RH defrost vent duct.
    • Remove the screw.
    RH defrost vent duct screw (E88636)
  3. Remove the RH vent duct.
    • Remove the clip.
    RH vent duct clip (E88637)
  4. Remove the center register duct.
    5. Center register duct (E88638)
  1. Remove the air inlet duct.
    • Remove the nut.
    Air inlet duct nut (E88647)
  2. Disconnect the defrost duct blend door control cable.
    • Release the clip.
    Defrost duct blend door control cable clip (E88650)
  3. Disconnect the footwell duct blend door control cable.
    • Release the clip.
    Footwell duct blend door control cable clip (E88651)
  4. Release the grommet from the bulkhead.
    5. Grommet from bulkhead (E88652)
  5. Disconnect the de-icing switch electrical connector.
De-icing switch electrical connector (E88653)
  1. Remove the heater core and evaporator core housing.
    • Remove the 4 nuts.
    Heater core and evaporator core housing nuts (E88654)

Installation

  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
    • Tighten to 15 Nm (11 lb.ft).
    Heater core and evaporator core housing installation (E88654)
  2. Tighten to 8 Nm (6 lb.ft).
    3. Tighten to 8 Nm (E88649)
  1. Connect the battery ground cable.
    For additional information, refer to Battery Connect
