Climate Control Assembly (80.10.02)

Removal

  1. Remove the instrument panel console.
    For additional information, refer to Instrument Panel Console (76.25.03)
  2. Release the climate control assembly.
    • Remove the 4 screws.
    Climate control assembly screws (E88469)
  3. Disconnect the blower motor speed control switch electrical connector.
    4. Blower motor speed control switch electrical connector (E91442)
  4. Disconnect the recirculation blend door control cable.
    • Release the clip.
    Recirculation blend door control cable clip (E91444)
  5. Disconnect the heater control valve control cable.
    • Release the clip.
Heater control valve control cable clip (E91443)
  1. Disconnect the climate control assembly illumination electrical connector.
    2. Climate control assembly illumination electrical connector (E91445)
  2. Remove the climate control assembly.
    • Release the clips.
    • Disconnect the 2 blend door control cables.
    Climate control assembly clips and blend door cables (E91446)

Installation

  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.

Blower Motor Resistor

Removal

  1. Disconnect the electrical connector.
    2. Blower motor resistor electrical connector (E89354)
  2. Remove the blower motor resistor.
    • Remove the 2 screws.
    Blower motor resistor screws (E88964)

Installation

  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
