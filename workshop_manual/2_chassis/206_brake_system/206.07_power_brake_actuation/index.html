Brake Booster (70.50.01)

Published: Jan 26, 2007

Removal

  1. Disconnect the battery ground cable.
    For additional information, refer to Battery Disconnect and Connect.
  2. Release the air conditioning (A/C) high-pressure pipe.
    • Remove the bolt.
    E89422
  3. Release the brake booster vacuum hose.
    E89423
  4. Disconnect the 2 electrical connectors from the brake fluid low level warning indicator.
    E89424
  5. Release the brake master cylinder.
    • Remove the 2 nuts.
E89425
  1. NOTE: RH shown, LH similar.
    Remove the 2 grommets from the brake pedal box.
    E89426
  2. Remove the brake booster clevis pin.
    • Remove and discard the split pin.
    E89427
  3. NOTE: RH shown, LH similar.
    Remove the brake booster.
    • Remove the 2 nuts.
E89428

Installation

  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
    • Tighten to 14 Nm (10 lb.ft).
    E89428
  2. Tighten to 26 Nm (19 lb.ft).
    E89425
  3. Connect the battery ground cable.
    For additional information, refer to Battery Connect.

Brake Booster Non-Return Valve

Published: Jun 14, 2006

Removal

  1. Disconnect brake vacuum hose from booster non return valve.
  2. Carefully prise valve out with a screwdriver blade between valve and grommet. Take care not to exert too much pressure on the vacuum chamber.
  3. Remove rubber grommet but be careful not to allow it to fall into the vacuum chamber.
  4. Check the valve for correct operation; it should not be possible to pass air through into the booster in direction of arrow. Do not use compressed air.
E82218

Installation

  1. Install rubber grommet.
  2. Smear ribs of the valve with Lucas Girling rubber grease to assist assembly, and push valve fully home.
  3. Connect vacuum hose to the valve.
  4. Road test vehicle.

Brake Vacuum Pump (70.50.19)

Published: Jan 24, 2007

Removal

  1. Disconnect the battery ground cable.
    For additional information, refer to Battery Disconnect and Connect.
  2. Remove the cooling fan.
    For additional information, refer to Cooling Fan (26.25.19).
  3. Release the tension from the accessory drive belt.
    • Rotate the accessory drive belt tensioner clockwise.
    E85993
  4. Disconnect the brake vacuum pump vacuum pipe.
    E85748
  5. CAUTION: Make sure that all openings are sealed. Use new blanking caps.
    Remove the brake vacuum pump.
    • Remove the 3 bolts.
    • Remove and discard the gasket.
    E85749

Installation

  1. NOTE: Remove and discard the blanking caps.
    NOTE: Clean the component mating faces.
    NOTE: Install a new gasket.
    Install the brake vacuum pump.
    • Tighten to 23 Nm (17 lb.ft).
    E85749
  2. Connect the brake vacuum pump vacuum pipe.
  3. Secure the accessory drive belt.
    • Rotate the accessory drive belt tensioner clockwise.
  4. Install the cooling fan.
    For additional information, refer to Cooling Fan (26.25.19).
  5. Connect the battery ground cable.
    For additional information, refer to Battery Connect.
