Steering Column (57.40.01)

Removal

NOTE: Make sure that the wheels and tires are in the straight-ahead position.
  1. Disconnect the battery ground cable.
    For additional information, refer to Battery Disconnect and Connect.
  2. Remove the instrument panel.
    For additional information, refer to Instrument Panel (76.46.23/99).
  3. Remove the steering column lock and ignition switch housing.
    For additional information, refer to Steering Column Lock and Ignition Switch Housing (57.40.31).
  4. Release the central junction box (CJB) from the bulkhead.
    • Remove the 4 nuts.
    E89436
  5. Release the steering column.
    • Remove the bolt.
    E89429
  6. Remove the brake pedal and bracket.
    For additional information, refer to Brake Pedal and Bracket (70.35.03).
  7. Release the steering column from the lower shaft.
    • Mark the relationship between both shafts.
    • Remove and discard the bolt.
E89430
  1. Release the steering column from the vehicle body.
    • Remove the 2 bolts.
    E89431
  2. Release the heater control valve and bracket.
    • Remove the 2 bolts.
    E89432
  3. Remove the steering column upper support bracket.
    • Remove the 4 bolts.
    • Collect the rubber strip from around the steering column shaft.
    E89433
  1. Remove the steering column from the steering column lower shaft.
    E89434
  2. Release the steering column main support bracket.
    • Remove the bolt.
    E89435
  3. NOTE: The steering column must be positioned correctly to pass through the bulkhead.
    Remove the steering column.
    E89437

Installation

CAUTION: Care must be taken when installing the steering column to the steering column lower shaft, failure to follow this instruction may result in damage to the steering column lower shaft.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
    • Tighten to 22 Nm (16 lb.ft).
E89435
  1. Tighten to 22 Nm (16 lb.ft).
    E89431
  2. Install the steering column upper support bracket.
    • Tighten the M6 bolts to 9 Nm (7 lb.ft).
    • Tighten the M8 bolts to 22 Nm (16 lb.ft).
    E89433
  3. Tighten to 22 Nm (16 lb.ft).
    E89432
  4. NOTE: Install a new bolt.

Tighten to 30 Nm (22 lb.ft).

E89430
  1. Tighten to 48 Nm (35 lb.ft).
    E89429
  2. Connect the battery ground cable.
    For additional information, refer to Battery Connect.

Steering Column Lower Shaft (57.40.16 / 57.40.27)

Removal

NOTE: Make sure that the wheels and tires are in the straight-ahead position.
  1. Release the steering column lower shaft.
    • Remove and discard the 2 bolts.
    E89488
  2. Remove the steering column lower shaft.
    • Mark the relationship between both shafts.
    • Remove and discard the bolt.
    E89430

Installation

  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
    • Tighten to 30 Nm (22 lb.ft).
    E89430
  1. Tighten to 30 Nm (22 lb.ft).
    E89488

Steering Wheel (57.61.01)

Special Service Tools

E90668

Remover, Steering Wheel 211-295

Removal

NOTE: Make sure that the wheels and tires are in the straight-ahead position.
  1. Remove the steering wheel center pad.
    E88430
  2. Remove the steering wheel nut and locking washer.
    E88431
  3. Mark the relationship between the steering wheel and steering column.
    E88432
  1. Using the special tool, remove the steering wheel.
    E90771

Installation

  1. NOTE: Make sure the alignment marks are correctly aligned.
    Install the steering wheel.
    • Tighten to 43Nm (32 lb.ft).
    E88431
  2. Install the steering wheel center pad.
